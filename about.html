ABOUT CLASSPULSE

A classroom that listens.

ClassPulse is a student learning feedback system designed to help teachers understand what students actually understand — before moving to the next concept.

THE PROBLEM

Teachers can't always see what the whole class understands.

In a normal classroom, a teacher may ask, "Is everything clear?" Most students may stay silent even when they are confused about a topic.

01

Students may hesitate to ask questions.

02

Teachers may not know which topic needs revision.

03

Understanding is difficult to measure during a normal lecture.

THE SOLUTION

Feedback becomes actionable.

ClassPulse turns simple student feedback into topic-level classroom insights.

01

Student Reviews

Students rate their understanding of individual topics from 0% to 100%.

→
02

Data is Analysed

ClassPulse calculates topic-level understanding from the submitted feedback.

→
03

Teacher Acts

Teachers can identify weak topics and decide what should be revised next.

KEY FEATURES

Built around real classroom needs.

01

Topic-Level Feedback

Students can rate individual topics instead of giving one general classroom rating.

02

Anonymous Feedback

Teachers can focus on learning patterns without displaying student identities.

03

Learning Analytics

Feedback is converted into understandable topic-level analytics.

04

Difficult Topic Detection

Students can directly identify concepts they are struggling with.

05

Revision Priority

Lower understanding scores help teachers identify where revision may be needed.

06

Teacher Insights

ClassPulse converts feedback into simple, actionable recommendations.

PRIVACY BY DESIGN

Feedback should feel safe.

ClassPulse is designed so that teachers can understand classroom learning patterns without exposing student names on the analytics dashboard.

✓ Anonymous review IDs
✓ No student names in analytics
✓ Learning-focused data
✓ Minimal information collection
