ABOUT CLASSPULSE

Feedback that turns into better learning.

ClassPulse is a student learning feedback platform designed to help teachers understand what their class actually understands — not just what was taught.

THE PROBLEM

What happens in a normal classroom?

01

Students stay silent

Many students hesitate to ask questions even when they have not understood a topic completely.

02

Teachers cannot see everyone

A teacher may explain a topic clearly, but it is difficult to know how every student understood it.

03

Weak topics remain hidden

Without regular feedback, difficult concepts can remain unnoticed until examinations.

OUR SOLUTION

One simple question: “Did you understand?”

ClassPulse gives students a simple way to report their understanding topic by topic. Teachers then receive meaningful class-level insights that help them decide what needs revision.

HOW IT WORKS

From classroom feedback to action.

ClassPulse keeps the process simple for both students and teachers.

01

Choose the subject

Students select the engineering subject and chapter they want to review.

02

Rate each subtopic

Students choose 0%, 25%, 50%, 75% or 100% based on their understanding.

03

Share confusion

Students can optionally explain what they found difficult or confusing.

04

Teacher gets insight

The dashboard highlights weak areas and recommended revision priorities.

KEY FEATURES

Built around the classroom.

📊

Topic-wise Analytics

Understand exactly which topics are strong and which need attention.

🔒

Anonymous Feedback

Students can share honest feedback without their identity being displayed normally.

💡

Recommended Action

ClassPulse highlights where teachers should focus their next revision.

DESIGN THINKING

Built using the Design Thinking process.

01

Empathize

Understand students' difficulty in expressing confusion and teachers' difficulty in identifying weak areas.

02

Define

Define the core problem: teachers need a simple way to see what students actually understand.

03

Ideate

Explore topic ratings, anonymous feedback, analytics and revision tracking.

04

Prototype

Build a working digital platform connecting student feedback with teacher insights.

05

Test

Test the platform with real classroom users and improve the experience using their feedback.

🔒

Privacy is part of the design.

ClassPulse is designed around anonymous classroom analytics. Student identity and learning feedback should remain separated during normal use.
