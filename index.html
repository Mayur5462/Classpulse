HOW IT WORKS

From classroom feedback to better teaching.

ClassPulse creates a simple feedback loop between students and teachers without interrupting the learning process.

01

Student Responds

Students select their subject, chapter and subtopics, then rate how well they understood each concept.

02

Feedback Gets Collected

Responses are organised topic-wise so teachers can quickly identify common learning difficulties.

03

Teacher Gets Insight

The dashboard highlights strong topics, weak topics and areas that may require revision.

04

Teaching Improves

Teachers can use the feedback to decide what should be explained, revised or practised next.
